## Scope

Single-page personal media portfolio. Experience mode: the work leads, the interface recedes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal media archive printed as a late-night newspaper: one front page that makes the work feel discovered, not uploaded.

OWN-WORLD: Warm newsprint (#e9e1cf), ink (#151515), oxidized red (#c23b2f), cobalt (#1f4b8f), and one acid-yellow highlight. Bodoni Moda display, Source Serif 4 body, Archivo Narrow labels. Thin rules, folio numbers, registration marks, halftone photographs, captions set tight under images. No generic cards, no glass, no gradient text.

STORY: The visitor lands on a dated edition, reads one strong photo essay, scans the contact sheet, watches a short motion study, plays an audio dispatch, then reads the personal note and finds a way to collaborate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width broadsheet above the fold: masthead RAIN SISTER across the top, issue line and live clock; a tall lead photograph in the left-center with a red EXCLUSIVE stamp; the headline "雨停之后，城市把光还给了我们" set in a large Chinese serif; a narrow right rail with the personal note, a contents list, and an audio player. The lead photo has a pointer-following reading-lamp mask as the signature interaction.

FORM: Direction 7 of 7: The Night Edition. Seed reference: seed:3bf00784a274b9d5.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
